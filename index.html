Casey Perdue

I'm a fifth year math PhD student at UC San Diego. My advisor is Claus Sorensen.

I’m broadly interested in algebraic number theory and p-adic representation theory. More specifically, I’m interested in studying mod-p representations of p-adic reductive groups and computing their cohomology groups, pos- sibly arising from computations involving Lie algebra cohomology, as well as understanding Hecke algebras associated to Serre weights of such p-adic reductive groups.
